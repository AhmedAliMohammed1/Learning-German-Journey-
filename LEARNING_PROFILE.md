# Learning profile

## Confirmed preferences

- Explain German grammar and corrections in simple **Egyptian Arabic**.
- Use **full-sentence translation**, primarily Egyptian Arabic → German; avoid default gap-filling exercises.
- Mix old and new material in cumulative reviews.
- Introduce useful new vocabulary in every review batch through practical daily-life sentences.
- Correct each submitted sentence and explain the important reason briefly.
- Review introduced verbs as existing material. Do not repeatedly teach them as new.
- When introducing a group of new verbs, mix those verbs with older material in practice.
- Begin new study days with a short review before new material.
- Track recurring mistakes, revisit them naturally, and reduce repetition as independent performance improves.
- Preserve exact continuation points and adapt to the learner's current requests.

## Correction format

1. Quote the learner's full answer.
2. Give a correct, natural full German sentence; acknowledge other valid versions.
3. Explain the relevant rule in Egyptian Arabic.
4. If useful, give one fresh sentence testing the same point with different vocabulary.
5. Record the original attempt and whether hints were needed.

Do not display the answer before the learner attempts the translation unless requested. A copied corrected answer is not independent mastery evidence.
