There is **no fixed final verb target**. The number above is only the current count of verbs that have actually been introduced/learned so far. Every future new verb is appended to this same inventory with the next sequential ID.

**Counting rule:** one unified verb list. There is no separate "numbered" vs "supplemental" count anymore. Historical IDs 1–100 are preserved for continuity, and previously supplemental verbs now continue from 101 onward. An entry being introduced does **not** mean it is mastered.

## Learning policy

- New verbs continue indefinitely as useful material appears; there is no target such as 100/100.
- New verbs receive the next sequential ID in this same table.
- Teach every newly introduced verb with **Präsens + Perfekt**, plus separability, case, or required prepositions where relevant.
- Present/context and Perfekt evidence remain separate; a correct form in one dimension does not prove mastery of the other.
- Full and Targeted review draw from this entire unified inventory according to the learner's selected scope and saved evidence.
- Historical status/evidence remains in `progress.json`; this table is the readable curriculum view.

## Migration note — 2026-10-09

At the learner's explicit request, the old fixed **100-verb target** and the separate **supplemental verb tables** were retired. All already learned verbs were merged into this single open-ended inventory without deleting previous evidence or changing mastery claims.
