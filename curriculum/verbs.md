# Verb curriculum — 167 introduced verbs

There is **no fixed final verb target**. This is one open-ended verb inventory. Every future new verb gets the next sequential ID.

**Important:** the forms and examples below are teaching references. They do **not** mean the learner has independently mastered every form.

## How to read the table

- **Präsens:** useful present-tense reference; important irregular forms are shown when helpful.
- **Perfekt:** auxiliary + participle. For modal verbs, the common double-infinitive pattern is noted when another infinitive is present.
- **Example:** a practical model sentence for meaning and structure.
- **Status:** learning status from actual evidence, separate from the reference forms.
- **Usage / note:** case, preposition, separability, reflexive pattern, or another key rule.

## Learning policy

- New verbs continue indefinitely; there is no final count goal.
- Every new verb is appended to this same table.
- Every new verb must be taught with **Präsens + Perfekt + a practical example + essential usage note**.
- Present/context and Perfekt evidence remain separate.
- Reference forms/examples are curriculum information, not retroactive proof of mastery.
- Full and Targeted review draw from this whole unified inventory.
