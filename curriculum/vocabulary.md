## Important forms already encountered

- **eine Tasse Wasser / Kaffee** = كمية مشروب في كوب. Wassertasse / Kaffeetasse usually labels a kind of cup; use the quantity expression for the intended meaning.
- die Menschen is the plural form encountered; singular der Mensch.
- die Eltern is plural in this meaning.
- der Name → den Namen; der Nachbar → den Nachbarn.
- morgen = بكرة; der Morgen = الصبح.
- die Bank here means financial institution; its plural is die Banken.
